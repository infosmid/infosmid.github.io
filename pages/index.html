Ownership & Leadership

About Infosmid (Pty) Ltd

Infosmid (Pty) Ltd is an independent software consulting firm owned and founded by Corneil du Plessis, based in Sandton, Gauteng, South Africa.

Corneil is a distinguished Principal Software Architect and former VMware/Broadcom Staff Engineer with extensive experience in software development across various scales—from embedded interactive systems to enterprise mainframes and cloud-native platforms.

Throughout his career—spanning leadership roles at Orix Data Dynamics, VMware & Broadcom, JumpCo, and Standard Bank—Corneil has delivered mission-critical architectures across financial services, insurance, and enterprise data management. A committer on the global Spring Cloud Data Flow project, long-standing JoziJUG co-organizer, international speaker, and technical author on Baeldung, he combines rigorous architectural patterns with pragmatic delivery.
