# Brand Strategy & Visual Hierarchy

## 1. Executive Brand Architecture & Strategy

The brand system operates under an **Endorsed Brand Architecture**, structuring the relationship between corporate authority and product-level high-performance utility:

* **Infosmid (The Parent Enterprise Anchor)**: Represents the institutional engine of intelligence ("Info" + "Smith/Forge"). The visual identity conveys architectural permanence, structural integrity, enterprise trust, and foundational governance.
* **Anvil (The Platform Product)**: Named directly after the tool of physical transformation and creation. The branding conveys modern high-precision engineering, industrial durability, and graph-centric intelligence, reinforcing how raw systems are forged into canonical business taxonomies.
* **Endorsement Relationship ("Anvil by Infosmid")**: Anvil operates as the flagship high-velocity operational platform, anchored and certified by Infosmid’s foundational governance and enterprise trust.

## 2. Dual-Mode Color Palette System

To maximize visual utility, ergonomic comfort, and contrast accessibility across varying operational environments, the brand establishes a **Dual-Mode System**:
- **Dark Mode (Industrial Forge Adaptation)**: Engineered for dark-mode workflow tools, graph visualization, and ontology modeling. Utilizes deep carbon bases with blue-grey metal and polished steel highlights to focus developer attention and minimize ocular fatigue during extended sessions.
- **Light Mode (Enterprise Precision)**: Engineered for high-clarity reporting, executive dashboards, and enterprise governance workflows. Utilizes clean warm off-white bases with cobalt blue and emerald accents to maximize reading speed and corporate alignment.

### Core Brand Color Tokens

| Token Role | Dark Mode (Industrial Forge) | Light Mode (Enterprise Precision) | Functional Purpose & Utility |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Base / Canvas** | `#141413` (Carbon Charcoal) | `#FAF9F5` (Warm Off-White) | Primary canvas background; minimizes glare while maintaining high contrast. |
| **Surface / Card** | `#1A202C` (Deep Slate Slate) | `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) | Elevates content layers, cards, and modal dialogs above canvas. |
| **Surface Alt / Container** | `#2A2F3D` (Muted Steel Slate) | `#F1F5F9` (Light Slate Tint) | Subtle groupings, table header backgrounds, and container regions. |
| **Primary Accent** | `#4A6D8C` / `#7B9BB7` (Blue-Grey Metal / Polished Steel) | `#1E40AF` (Cobalt Blue) | Primary interactive actions, active navigation, key data transformations. |
| **Supporting Neutral** | `#4A5568` / `#B0AEA5` (Steel Slate / Mid Gray) | `#0F766E` / `#788C5D` (Emerald Slate / Sage) | Secondary actions, structural lines, borders, and contextual metadata. |
| **Subtle Neutral / Border** | `#334155` (Slate Gray) | `#E8E6DC` / `#E2E8F0` (Light Gray) | Non-intrusive container borders, grid lines, and dividers. |

| **Secondary Text** | `#B0AEA5` (Warm Muted Gray) | `#475569` (Slate Gray) | Secondary labels, descriptions, and supporting captions. |

## 4. Dual-Tier Typographic Hierarchy

To balance corporate brand presence with the high information density required by technical dashboards, graph visualizers, and data health tables, the typography system is divided into two purposeful tiers:

### Typography Tiers

1. **Brand & Display Tier (Headings & Corporate Identity)**:
   - **Primary Font**: `Poppins`, sans-serif (Headings 24pt / 32px and larger, section banners, marketing collateral).
   - **Fallback**: `Arial`, sans-serif.
   - **Characteristics**: Geometric, open, modern, and structural. Communicates high-precision engineering.
2. **Editorial & Brand Narrative Tier (Documentation & Marketing Copy)**:
   - **Primary Font**: `Lora`, serif (Long-form corporate narratives, whitepapers, case studies).
   - **Fallback**: `Georgia`, serif.
   - **Characteristics**: High editorial legibility, balanced contrast, humanized warmth.
3. **Application & Technical UI Tier (Data Grids, Node Labels, Controls)**:
   - **Primary Font**: `Roboto`, `Roboto Flex`, sans-serif (Body text, data tables, graph nodes, telemetry badges, form fields).
   - **Fallback**: `Arial`, `Helvetica`, sans-serif.
   - **Characteristics**: Compact tabular figures, high horizontal efficiency, optimized for dense information displays and WebGL/SVG diagram rendering.

### Typographic Scale

| Level | Size | Weight | Line Height | Font Family | Usage Context |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Display / H1** | 32px (24pt+) | SemiBold (600) | 1.25 | `Poppins`, Arial, sans-serif | Platform title, major dashboard headers |
| **H2** | 24px (18pt) | Medium (500) | 1.3 | `Poppins`, Arial, sans-serif | Modal titles, primary section headers |
| **H3** | 18px (14pt) | Medium (500) | 1.4 | `Roboto`, sans-serif | Card headings, drawer headers |
| **Body (Default)** | 16px (12pt) | Regular (400) | 1.5 | `Roboto`, sans-serif | Primary body copy, table text, descriptions |
| **Body (Dense/Grid)** | 14px (10.5pt) | Regular (400) | 1.4 | `Roboto`, sans-serif | High-density tables, tree views, metadata |
| **Caption / Badge** | 12px (9pt) | Medium (500) | 1.3 | `Roboto Flex`, Roboto, sans-serif | Status chips, diagram node sub-labels, timestamps |
| **Code / Monospace** | 13px (10pt) | Regular (400) | 1.4 | `JetBrains Mono`, monospace | JSON viewers, schema definitions, SQL queries |

## 5. Logo Concepts & Visual Assets

### Canonical Logo Assets
All canonical vector assets are maintained in the platform repository under `/images/`:

* **Infosmid Corporate Mark**:
  - Square Icon: `infosmid-icon-square.svg`  and for light mode: `infosmid-icon-light.svg` and dark mode: `infosmid-icon-dark.svg`
  - Horizontal Lockup: `infosmid-logo-horizontal.svg` and for light mode: `infosmid-logo-horizontal-light.svg` and dark mode: `infosmid-logo-horizontal-dark.svg`
  - **Design Concept ("The Data Strata & Info-Forge")**: An architectural mark comprising structured horizontal strata and interlocking geometric segments that form a stylized 'I' and 'S'. It symbolizes raw information layers forged into an unyielding corporate foundation. Default mark fill uses Deep Charcoal (`#1A202C`) on light backgrounds and Warm White (`#FAF9F5`) on dark surfaces.
* **Anvil Product Mark**:
  - Square Icon: `anvil-icon-square.svg` and for light mode: `anvil-icon-light.svg` and dark mode: `anvil-icon-dark.svg`
  - Horizontal Lockup: `anvil-logo-horizontal.svg` and for light mode: `anvil-logo-horizontal-light.svg` and dark mode: `anvil-logo-horizontal-dark.svg` 
  - **Design Concept ("The Node Anvil")**: A precision blacksmith's anvil silhouette where vertices, intersections, and structural focal points are articulated by interconnected graph nodes. This directly symbolizes the transformation of raw operational schemas into high-value knowledge graphs and canonical business ontologies.

### Clearspace and Sizing Rules
* **Clearspace**: Maintain a minimum exclusion zone around logos equal to half the height of the anvil mark ($0.5H$) free of competing text or graphics.
* **Minimum Sizing**:
  - Horizontal lockup: Minimum height of `28px` in digital interfaces.
  - Square icon: Minimum dimensions of `16px x 16px` (favicon) or `24px x 24px` (navigation bars).
* **High-Contrast Rendering**:
  - On dark backgrounds (`#141413` / `#1A202C`), render the mark using `anvil-logo-light.svg` or fill `#FAF9F5`.
  - On light backgrounds (`#FAF9F5` / `#FFFFFF`), render using `anvil-logo-light.svg` of fill `#1A202C` or primary accent `#1E40AF`.

## 6. Icon Suite & Shape System

The platform visual language uses unified geometric iconography across tooling:

* **Systems / Sources**: Cylinder / Database node (Slate `#94A3B8` / `#475569`).
* **Taxonomies**: Hierarchy tree / Business shield (Cyan `#00E5FF` / `#0284C7`).
* **Ontologies**: Dual-node mapping bridge (Violet `#8B5CF6` / `#6D28D9`).
* **Workflows / Processes**: Interlocking directional arrows (Blue-Grey Metal `#4A6D8C` / `#7B9BB7` or Cobalt `#1E40AF`).

### Shape & Accent Styling Rules
* **Corners & Radii**:
  - Cards, modals, and large panels: `8px` (`rounded-lg`).
  - Buttons, chips, and input fields: `4px` (`rounded`).
  - Graph nodes and status indicators: fully circular (`rounded-full`) or faceted pill shapes.
* **Borders & Elevation**:
  - In dark mode, rely on 1px subtle borders (`#334155`) with low-intensity ambient shadows rather than heavy drop shadows.
  - In light mode, use clean neutral borders (`#E2E8F0` / `#E8E6DC`) with crisp, low-blur elevation shadows.

## 7. Technical Implementation & Tooling Alignment

### Angular Material v21 Theme Token Integration
Tokens defined in `../anvil-app-ui/src/themes/tokens.scss` directly embody this brand strategy:
* `$dark-theme-overrides`: Implements Industrial Forge dark palette with Blue-Grey Metal/Cyan primary accents and carbon bases (`#0F172A` / `#141413`).
* `$light-theme-overrides`: Implements Enterprise Precision light palette with Cobalt Blue (`#0284C7` / `#1E40AF`), Slate neutrals, and `#FAF9F5` canvas backgrounds.
* `$typography-overrides`: Binds `Roboto` for base and dense UI components, with `Poppins` injected into heading components.

### Tailwind CSS Utility Layer
Utility classes align with token roles:
* `bg-primary`, `text-primary`: Primary brand accents.
* `bg-surface-container`, `border-outline`: Structural boundaries.
* Spacing scale: Standard 4px, 8px, 16px, 24px grid (`p-2`, `p-4`, `gap-2`, `gap-4`).

### Charts & Visualizations (ngx-echarts & ng-diagram)
* **Tooltips**: Always dark background (`#212121`) with clean white text (`#FAF9F5`) across both light and dark themes to preserve contrast over multi-colored data series.
* **Series Accents**: Use consistent semantic color accents (Primary, Secondary, Tertiary) for data series matching the semantic tier rules.
